About

Subwiid

Minimal house that strips down, hard techno that hits back.

subwiid is Jacques Wud, a South African DJ turning up the pressure from minimal house through to hard techno. His sets strip the floor bare: starting deep and tight, bar by bar, until the room runs on hard techno alone.

Every set is mixed live and read off the room, built for dark rooms, never on autopilot. TikTok (@subwiid) is the growth engine, with raw clips from the bedroom to the floor captured as it happens. SoundCloud carries the mixes and edits; originals in production.

He travels anywhere in South Africa for the right room and fee. Now taking first bookings.
